Bounty Board DAO

Get work done, pay on‑chain. A decentralized bounty board on Cardano — post a task, fund it in ADA, and pay the contributor with a real blockchain transaction the moment you approve their work.

Source Code

Use Case & Problem Solved

Getting paid for online work still runs on trust. Contributors do the work first and hope the poster pays; posters pay first and hope the work arrives. Freelance platforms take large cuts and hold funds, and community/DAO bounties live in scattered spreadsheets and chat threads with no verifiable record of who was paid.


Bounty Board DAO turns the payout itself into a blockchain transaction. No middleman, no custody of funds, and wallet‑native identity via the CIP‑30 standard (Eternl, Lace, Nami, Yoroi, and more).


Key Features:

  • Wallet connect for any installed CIP‑30 wallet (Eternl, Lace, Nami, Yoroi)
  • Post & fund bounties denominated in ADA
  • Claim flow with a note field for work delivery links
  • Approve & pay — builds, signs, and submits a real sendLovelace transaction on Preprod
  • Network and balance guardrails before every payout
  • Verifiable receipts — each paid bounty links to Cardanoscan

Project Description

Bounty Board DAO is a full‑stack Web3 application built on Cardano's Preprod testnet. The frontend is built with Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript, while the Web3 layer uses the Mesh SDK for CIP‑30 wallet interactions and transaction building.


A poster connects their wallet and lists a task with an ADA reward. Contributors connect their wallet, browse open bounties, and submit a claim with a note linking their work. The poster reviews the claim and clicks Approve & pay — the app builds a transaction that pays the contributor directly. The poster signs in their wallet, and the payment settles on‑chain with a public, verifiable receipt on Cardanoscan.


The payout logic uses the Mesh SDK's Transaction builder to call sendLovelace with the reward amount. Guardrails check the network (Preprod vs. mainnet) and ensure the poster's balance can cover the reward + fees before signing.


Key Concepts Implemented:
• CIP‑30 Wallet Integration (Eternl, Lace, Nami, Yoroi)
• Mesh SDK & Blockfrost Provider
• Transaction building and signing on Cardano
• Next.js App Router (server components + client components)
• In‑memory store (easily swappable for a database)
• Real‑time bounty status updates
• Preprod testnet deployment
